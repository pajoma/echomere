# Echomere

A meditative deep-sea screensaver where whales travel anonymously from cove to cove around the world,
connecting people who never meet.

## Language

**Creature**:
An animal a person creates and sends on trips. In the first version every creature is a whale.
_Avoid_: Pet, animal, whale (as the general term)

**Ambient life**:
The fish, plankton and other background life that fills the scene. Nobody owns it and it never travels.
_Avoid_: Creatures, NPCs

**Creator**:
The person who made a creature and sent it out. Each creator has exactly one creature. Creators are
anonymous: they're known only by their rough location and the traits they picked.
_Avoid_: User, owner, keeper, player

**Cove**:
A town with an oceanic name, such as "Munich Bay", that holds up to five whales at a time. Every town on the
list is a cove, whether or not anyone lives there. All creators who live there share it, and each of their
screens shows the creatures visiting it. A creature's **home cove** is the cove it started from, and it stays
its home forever.
_Avoid_: Ocean, installation, host, peer

**Watched cove**:
A cove where at least one creator is still watching.

**Quiet cove**:
A cove where nobody is watching, either because no creator lives there or because they have all stopped
looking. Creatures still visit it, but only when the watched coves nearby are full.
_Avoid_: Abandoned cove, empty cove

**Echo**:
The trace a creature leaves in a cove after it has moved on. It fades after a few days.
_Avoid_: Ghost, orphan

**Neighbour**:
A nearby cove with room that a creature can move to next. Watched coves come first; quiet coves count only
when the watched coves around them are full.
_Avoid_: Peer, contact

**Deep sea**:
The open water outside every cove. Creatures cross it on long journeys between distant coves and wait there
when every cove nearby is full. Nobody can watch it.
_Avoid_: Limbo, queue

**Gesture**:
A fixed, wordless reaction that someone watching a visiting creature sends to its creator: Bubbles, Song or
Light.
_Avoid_: Like, message, reaction

**Trip**:
A creature's endless journey from cove to cove, one cove at a time and one day per stop. A creature
comes home only by chance.
_Avoid_: Broadcast, migration

**Travel log**:
The list of places a creature has visited on its trip. Its creator can see it, and so can anyone whose screen
it is visiting.
_Avoid_: History, route
