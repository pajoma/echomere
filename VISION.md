# Echomere: Vision

Echomere is a meditative deep-sea screensaver. Each person creates one whale and sends it on an endless trip
from cove to cove around the world. A cove is a town, shared by every creator who lives there, and every
screen in it looks into the same water, like a webcam below the surface. A whale stays one day at each stop,
leaves an echo behind when it moves on, and collects gestures (Bubbles, Song, Light) from the people who
watched it in its travel log.

People who never meet are connected through the whales that visit them. There are no accounts, no names, no
free text and no moderation: only places, a few chosen traits, and wordless gestures.

The scene is realistic, dark and slow, and it uses hardly any energy. Echomere is a hobby project under the
MIT licence, macOS first.

The terms are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md), the decisions in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/), and the full
